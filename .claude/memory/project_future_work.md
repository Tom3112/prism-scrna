Perturbation prediction is the natural next step after cell type annotation, and should be pitched as future work in the Immunai interview.

**Why:** Immunai's core value proposition includes in silico drug screening — predicting how drugs/knockouts shift cell expression profiles without wet lab experiments. Models like GEARS and scGPT (perturbation mode) do this by pretraining on unperturbed scRNA data (what we built) then fine-tuning on paired before/after perturbation datasets.

**How to apply:** When discussing the project, frame cell type annotation as the foundation and perturbation prediction as the compelling next step that ties directly to Immunai's drug discovery use case.
